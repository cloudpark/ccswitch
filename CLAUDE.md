# ccswitch

This is a **public, open-source project** (hosted on GitHub: ksred/ccswitch). Treat everything in this repo as visible to the public.

## Critical rule: no internal/private content

**Never commit, add, or reference any internal Cloudpark information in this repo.** This includes but is not limited to:

- Internal tooling, systems, credentials, API keys, or infrastructure details
- Internal project names, codenames, client names, or business data
- Internal Slack messages, tickets, docs, or other non-public context
- Personal or company email addresses, tokens, or other secrets

If a task or instruction seems to originate from internal Cloudpark context, keep the resulting code/docs generic and free of any identifying internal references before committing here. When in doubt, ask before committing.
